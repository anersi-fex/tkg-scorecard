## What this is
A sibling of Sales Coach (`anersi-fex/tkg-sales-coach`) with the same Tide look: a TV board for the office (a Mac mini on HDMI) showing the team's month, and a small phone app to feed it. Release 1 (2026-10-27, built overnight for a 7 AM deadline): the board (`/board`), This month (`/`, earlier months with `?month=`), Log a sale (`/log`, `?agent=<id>` preselects a person for a desk NFC tag), Team (`/team`), Settings (`/settings`, owner only), a Shortcut endpoint (`POST /api/sales` with `LOG_TOKEN`). The Wrap (`/wrap`, `?day=`, 2026-10-29; owner: "super screenshotable and tells you a lot on one screenshot" for the group chat): one day on one iPhone screenshot, the player of the day with the best deal, everybody's day (no-sale rows dimmed; past seven people the no-sale names fold into one line), carrier counts, the month as it stood that day, and a verdict against what the goal needed that day (`src/lib/wrap.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/wrap.test.ts`: `neededOnDay` spreads what was left before the day over the business days from that day on, that day included, in whole dollars; Home's "a day" spreads what is left now over the days after today, so the two differ on purpose; a weekend has no verdict; the best deal is a single policy, an imported day's total only when nothing else was logged; the clock shows only for a sale logged on its own day; sized to end above the tab bar at 390x844 with everyone selling, checked by the smoke test). Mockups: `design/wrap-mockups.html` (`?s=A|B|C|D&t=light|dark`, rendered with Playwright at 390x844). The owner turned down award tiles, a month standings table and a talk-time entry (not until the dialer feeds it). Later, as the owner asked: deeper data from Sales Coach and the CRM (which leads, states, numbers, dial time), then everything folds into one CRM.

## Stack (match the owner's other apps; do not substitute)
- Next.js 16.3.4 App Router (TypeScript strict, Tailwind 4, React 19), `src/proxy.ts` (not middleware), async `params`/`searchParams`/`cookies()`. Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before a new convention.
- Supabase Postgres (its own project) through `@supabase/supabase-js` with the service role key, server only (`src/lib/supabase.ts`). The browser never talks to Supabase. RLS on with no policies plus revoked grants for anon/authenticated (`supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql`).
- Sign-in: two passwords, `OWNER_PASSWORD` (Anersi: full control) and `TEAM_PASSWORD` (everyone and the TV: log sales, see everything), checked in constant time (`src/lib/auth.ts` `roleForPassword`), HMAC-signed stateless cookie with the role and a password tag (`src/lib/session.ts`; changing a password signs out that role's devices), 180-day cookie, a per-address login throttle in `login_attempts` (10 in 15 minutes; a 500 flood cap across addresses), sign-out through the `logout` server action (origin-checked; there is no sign-out route). `requirePage()` in every page, `requireRole()` in every action; `requireRole("owner")` for goals, team edits, fixing or removing any sale. The team can take back a sale logged in the last 15 minutes.
- Data (`src/lib/data.ts`): `agents` (never deleted, `active` false = off the board), `sales` (cents, `carrier`, `sold_on` Detroit date, soft `deleted_at`), `goals` (team, per month), `agent_goals`, `settings` (key `app`: board message, product and carrier chips, colorway, TV look `boardTheme`), `login_attempts`.
- Math (`src/lib/month.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/month.test.ts`): Detroit dates, business days (Mon to Fri), `monthClock` (`elapsed` counts today, `remaining` is the business days after today, so the two add up to the month's business days; owner, 2026-10-27: "only weekdays, working days", a Tuesday shows 3 left; `todayBusiness` says whether today is a weekday of the month, and with `remaining` 0 that is the last business day, which `daysLeftLabel` shows as "Last business day"; a weekend after it reads "No business days left" with the amount "to go", since Saturday sales still count), `pace` (projected, expected on the business-day line to the goal, ahead/behind, needed per remaining day; the chart's goal line climbs by the same business days). `src/lib/board.ts` builds one `MonthSnapshot` for the board, Home and the recap (`buildSnapshot` is pure; leaderboard ranks by premium, then policies, then name; `sales` is the month's own list newest first, `recent` the newest across months for the TV feed, and `/api/board` sends `sales` empty).
- The board (`src/components/board/board-view.tsx`): one screen sized in `em` from the viewport (`clamp(10px, min(1vw, 1.78vh), 36px)`), polls `GET /api/board` every 5 s (and reloads itself once when `MonthSnapshot.build`, Vercel's commit id, changes, so a deploy reaches a TV that was opened weeks ago), celebrates each sale id it has not seen (queue, 9 s each, confetti from `confetti.ts`), light or dark by the owner's TV look (Settings > TV look, `settings.app.boardTheme`, `saveBoardTheme`; `MonthSnapshot.boardTheme` rides every poll, so a change reaches the TV within 5 s; `theme-script.ts` reads it from `<html data-board-theme>` before paint and ignores the browser's saved app theme on `/board`; Settings is the only control, nothing on the TV changes it and the address is ignored), F full screen, wake lock, cursor hides when still. `month-chart.tsx` measures its own box and draws in pixels (strokes, labels and the dot in em).
- Forms that can fail (fix a sale, goals, rename, board settings) submit through `onSubmit` + `new FormData` + `startTransition`, never a form `action` prop: React 19 resets a form after an action, which would wipe what the owner typed. The Log form sends `sold_on` only when the person picked a day; the server stamps its own Detroit today otherwise, and refuses a future day.
- Links the app hands out (desk links, the Shortcut address) come from `appBase()` in `src/lib/app-url.ts`: `APP_URL`, else the request's own host.
- Timezone: America/Detroit everywhere (`src/lib/timezone.ts`).

## How to run and check
- `npm install`, `npm run dev`. Before every push: `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` (`npx next typegen` first when routes change), `npm test`, `npm run build`.
- End to end: `scripts/local-db/start.sh --reset` (Postgres 16 + PostgREST stand-in, needs `/usr/lib/postgresql/16`), then build and start the app with the env in `.local-db/env` plus `OWNER_PASSWORD`, `TEAM_PASSWORD`, `LOG_TOKEN`, `APP_URL`, then `node scripts/smoke.mjs` (Playwright; screenshots in `test-results/`). Run it on a fresh database.
- Env vars: see `SETUP.md` (no `.env.example`; `.env*` is gitignored).
