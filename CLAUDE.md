## Rules
- The owner approves scope before code. Build small, verified steps. Nothing here is AI-scored or AI-generated; the numbers are what people typed.
- Privacy: a sale carries an agent, an annual premium, a kind (life or P&C, `0003_pc.sql`), the carrier it was written with (owner, 2026-10-27: AHL, AmAm, Americo, Foresters, Newbridge, Royal, UHL; the list lives in Settings > Carriers, `settings.app.carriers`; migration `0002_carrier.sql` adds `sales.carrier`; the P&C carriers with their lines in `settings.app.pcCarriers`) and an optional product (a P&C sale: the line). No client names (the `client` column stays, unused since 0002), policy numbers, birthdates or contact details. Logs carry ids and error classes only.
